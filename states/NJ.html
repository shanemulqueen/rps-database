Renewable Portfolio Standard

RPS NJ Board of Public Utilities (BPU) N.J.S.A. 48:3-87 Applies to: EDC, Retail Supplier
NJ's RPS covers Class I (new renewables) and Class II (existing resources). Solar has a separate carve-out tracked as a distinct compliance obligation. RPS currently targets 50% by 2030, with further increases under executive order toward 100% clean electricity by 2035.

Legislation

Electric Discount and Energy Competition Act (P.L. 1999, c. 23)  enacted 1999
Original RPS enactment; established Societal Benefits Charge for clean energy funding
2012 Solar Portfolio Standard Act (P.L. 2012, c. 24)
Mandated 4.1% solar by 2028; created SREC system; established Solar Alternative Compliance Payments
2018 An Act Concerning Clean Energy (P.L. 2018, c. 17)
Escalated RPS to 35% by 2025 and 50% by 2030; accelerated solar RPS; 600 MW storage by 2021
2021 An Act Concerning Certain Solar Energy Projects (P.L. 2021, c. 169)
Created SREC-II program targeting 3,750 MW by 2026; established competitive bidding for solar incentives

Solar Carve-Out / Successor Solar Incentive (SuSI)

RPS NJ Board of Public Utilities (BPU) N.J.S.A. 48:3-87(d) Applies to: EDC, Retail Supplier
NJ's solar program has evolved through three generations — SREC (2004–2020), TREC (2020–2021), and now SuSI (2021–present). SuSI has two tracks: the Administratively Determined Incentive (ADI) for smaller and community solar, and the Competitive Solar Incentive (CSI) for larger projects. Target is 5.1% solar by 2026 (approximately 3,750 MW). Solar RECs satisfy the solar carve-out separately from the general RPS.

Legislation

Solar Portfolio Standard Act (P.L. 2012, c. 24)  enacted 2012
Established formal SREC solar carve-out; mandated 4.1% solar by 2028
2018 An Act Concerning Clean Energy (P.L. 2018, c. 17)
Accelerated solar carve-out to 5.1% by 2021; created TREC as transition mechanism
2021 An Act Concerning Certain Solar Energy Projects (P.L. 2021, c. 169)
Created SuSI program (ADI + CSI tracks); SREC-II bridges to SuSI; 3,750 MW target by 2026

Procurement Requirements

SuSI ADI: Fixed 15-year incentive payments set administratively by BPU. Targets community solar, rooftop, and small commercial projects. SuSI CSI: Competitive solicitations for projects above the ADI size threshold. Winning projects receive 15-year contracts at bid prices. Administered through BPU competitive procurement rounds.

Offshore Wind — OREC Program

PROCUREMENT NJ Board of Public Utilities (BPU) N.J.S.A. 48:3-87.1 (Offshore Wind Economic Development Act) Applies to: EDC, Retail Supplier
NJ's offshore wind program uses Offshore Renewable Energy Certificates (ORECs) — a state-administered procurement, not a voluntary market. BPU selects projects through competitive solicitation rounds and sets OREC prices. EDCs are required to purchase ORECs from approved projects. Current goal is 11,000 MW by 2040 (per EO

Legislation

Offshore Wind Economic Development Act (P.L. 2010, c. 57)  enacted 2010
Created OREC mechanism; required minimum 1,100 MW procurement; authorized BPU implementing regulations
2018 An Act Concerning Clean Energy (P.L. 2018, c. 17)
Increased offshore wind goal to 3,500 MW by 2030 (later further raised by executive orders)

Procurement Requirements

BPU conducts competitive solicitation rounds. Applicants submit project proposals with OREC price bids. BPU selects winners based on economic and non-economic criteria including New Jersey economic benefits and supply chain commitments. Selected developers enter into 20-year OREC agreements with NJ EDCs. Community benefit agreements required for approved projects.
