Programs

Class I & Class IA RPS (New Renewables)

RPS ME MPUC 35-A M.R.S. § 3210 Applies to: CMP, Versant Power, Retail Supplier
Maine distinguishes Class I (general new renewables) and Class IA (post-2005 new renewables, ≤100 MW except wind/solar, with fish-passage requirements for hydro). Class IA carries a separate escalating target — 11% in 2025, increasing 1% annually to 50% by 2040 per the 2025 update (LD 1868). Combined RPS target is 90% renewable electricity by 2040 plus a new 10% Clean Energy Standard (Class III) effective 2031.

Legislation

An Act To Reform Maine's Renewable Portfolio Standard (LD 1494, P.L. 2019, Chapter 477)  enacted 2019
Set 80% renewable target by 2030; created Class IA category; mandated thermal renewable energy credits (TRECs); required long-term contracts for Class IA resources
2025 An Act To Advance A Clean Energy Economy (LD 1868, P.L. 2025, Chapter 386)
Updated target to 90% renewable by 2040; added 10% Clean Energy Standard (Class III) by 2040; increased Class IA to 50% by 2040 via 1%/year increases

Generation Requirements

Class IA hydro must have fish passage or demonstrate no barrier to anadromous fish. Class IA facilities must have commenced operation or been substantially refurbished after September 1, 2005. Long-term contracts (20-year terms) required for Class IA procurement per MPUC competitive solicitations.

Procurement Requirements

MPUC conducts competitive solicitations for Class IA resources (35-A M.R.S. § 3210-G). Solicitations weighted 70% ratepayer benefit, 30% economic benefit. Winning projects receive 20-year contracts. EDCs are required to seek Class IA contracts in each solicitation round.

Class II RPS (Existing Renewables)

RPS ME MPUC 35-A M.R.S. § 3210 Applies to: CMP, Versant Power, Retail Supplier
Class II covers existing renewable resources that do not qualify for Class I or IA due to vintage or size. Primarily captures existing large hydro and older biomass. Target is 30% from Class II under the updated 90% overall standard.

Legislation

35-A M.R.S. § 3210 (established with original RPS, 1999)  enacted 1999
Original Class II framework for existing renewable resources

Offshore Wind Procurement

PROCUREMENT ME DOER / ME MPUC 35-A M.R.S. § 3407–3408 Applies to: CMP, Versant Power
Maine's offshore wind program targets at least 3,000 MW by 2040 in the Gulf of Maine — a floating offshore wind market given water depths. The program specifically protects Lobster Management Area I. DOER (launched September 2025, replacing the Governor's Energy Office) leads solicitation; MPUC reviews contracts. Commercial solicitation originally due July 1, 2025; timeline being established.

Legislation

An Act Regarding the Procurement of Offshore Wind Energy Resources (LD 1895, P.L. 2023, Chapter 481)  enacted 2023
Authorized procurement of at least 3,000 MW by 2040; established Gulf of Maine focus; required protection of Lobster Management Area I; created $5,000/MW contribution to Offshore Wind Research Consortium

Procurement Requirements

DOER must file a commercial solicitation with MPUC for offshore wind contracts. Projects must demonstrate coordination with fisheries (especially lobster). Diversity, equity, and inclusion requirements for coastal and disadvantaged communities. $5,000 per MW contribution to the Offshore Wind Research Consortium. EDCs are required to enter contracts approved by MPUC.
