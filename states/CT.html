Class I RPS

RPS CT PURA C.G.S. § 16-245a Applies to: EDC, Retail Supplier
Class I covers new renewable resources that began commercial operation on or after July 1, 2003. Target escalates to 40% by 2030. Municipal electric utilities are subject to a separate, lower standard under § 16-245a(b).

Legislation

An Act Concerning Electric Restructuring (Public Act 98-28)  enacted 1998
Original RPS enactment; established Class I and Class II framework
2013 An Act Concerning Connecticut's Clean Energy Goals (Public Act 13-303)
Expanded Class I eligibility; prohibited interstate REC double-counting; authorized DEEP to issue competitive RFPs
2018 An Act Concerning Connecticut's Energy Future (Public Act 18-50)
Increased Class I target to 26% by 2023, escalating to 40% by 2030; replaced net metering with tariff-based programs

Class II RPS

RPS CT PURA C.G.S. § 16-245a Applies to: EDC, Retail Supplier
Class II covers older renewable and waste resources — primarily MSW, biomass, and hydro that predates the Class I commercial operation cutoff. A 4% Class II (or additional Class I) requirement applies.

Legislation

An Act Concerning Electric Restructuring (Public Act 98-28)  enacted 1998
Established Class II tier for pre-2004 eligible resources

Class III RPS

RPS CT PURA C.G.S. § 16-245a Applies to: EDC, Retail Supplier
Class III is a distinct efficiency and CHP standard, separate from the renewable tiers. Covers combined heat and power (non-renewable fuel), electricity savings from CHP, and conservation and load management programs. 4% requirement as of 2010.

Legislation

An Act Concerning Electric Restructuring (Public Act 98-28)  enacted 1998
Established Class III for CHP and efficiency resources

Generation Requirements

Class III certificates (C3RECs) are issued by NEPOOL GIS. CHP systems must meet efficiency standards. Conservation and load management savings are verified by PURA-approved measurement and verification protocols.

Zero-Carbon Procurement (Millstone)

PROCUREMENT CT DEEP / CT PURA C.G.S. § 16a-3m Applies to: Eversource CT, United Illuminating
Not a certificate/credit program. DEEP is authorized to solicit long-term power purchase agreements from zero-carbon generators. In 2019, Millstone Nuclear Power Station (Dominion) secured a 10-year PPA for ~9 million MWh/year (approx. 50% of output) at $49.99/MWh, running through 2029. Distribution companies are required to enter the resulting PPAs approved by PURA.

Legislation

An Act Concerning Zero Carbon Procurement (Public Act 17-3, Special Session)  enacted 2017
Authorized DEEP to solicit zero-carbon resource contracts; directed study of Millstone economic viability; established 12 million MWh annual cap

Procurement Requirements

DEEP issues competitive solicitations for zero-carbon electricity. Contracts are long-term PPAs (3–10 years for nuclear/hydro). PURA reviews and approves all selected contracts. Distribution companies take title to the energy under the approved PPAs. Total annual contracted volume capped at 12 million MWh.
