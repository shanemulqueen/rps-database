Programs

Renewable Portfolio Standard

RPS NH Department of Energy RSA 362-F Applies to: EDC, Retail Supplier
NH's RPS has four active classes (I, II, III, IV) plus a Class I Thermal sub-class. Class I covers new renewables; Class II is post-2006 solar with a solar-specific ACP; Class III is pre-2006 biomass/methane (≤25 MW); Class IV is pre-2006 small hydro (≤5 MW). Total target is 25.2% by 2025, one of the lower mandates in New England. Administration transferred from PUC to the new NH Department of Energy effective July 1, 2021.

Legislation

Electric Renewable Portfolio Standard (Laws of 2007, Chapter 26)  enacted 2007
Original RPS enactment establishing Class I, II, III, and IV framework
2012 Class I Thermal Amendment (Laws of 2012, Chapter 272)
Created Class I Thermal sub-class for useful thermal renewable energy (geothermal, solar thermal, biomass heat, methane heat); effective 2014; first NH-specific thermal REC program
2013 RPS Study Committee Amendments (Laws of 2013, Chapter 279)
Modified Class I, I Thermal, and Class III obligations; established RPS study committee
2017 Clean Energy Jobs and Opportunity Act (Laws of 2017, Chapter 226)
Increased Class III ACP from $45 to $55/REC; minimum 15% of residential REF funds to low-moderate income customers
2021 Administrative Transfer (HB 2, 2021)
Transferred RPS administration and Renewable Energy Fund from NH PUC to new NH Department of Energy, effective July 1, 2021

Generation Requirements

Class I Thermal RECs require metering, verification, and reporting of useful thermal energy output per DOE protocols. Maximum credit value of $29/MWh of useable thermal energy. All other classes: standard NEPOOL GIS REC issuance.

Technology Eligibility

TechnologyStatusConditions / Notes
Solar PV Eligible
Post-2006 solar qualifies as Class II with solar-specific Alternative Compliance Payment. Pre-2006 solar is rare but would be Class I.
Wind (Onshore) Eligible
All wind qualifies as Class I (new renewables).
Offshore Wind Eligible
Class I eligible. NH has no offshore water but offshore wind certificates from other New England states are eligible.
Hydro — Run of River Eligible
New run-of-river hydro qualifies as Class I. Existing facilities ≤5 MW operational before 2006 qualify as Class IV.
Hydro — Large / Conventional Conditional
Large hydro not eligible for Class I or Class IV. Only small hydro (≤5 MW, pre-2006) is eligible under Class IV.
Biomass (Solid) Eligible
New biomass facilities qualify as Class I. Pre-2006 biomass ≤25 MW qualifies as Class III with its own ACP schedule. Class I Thermal sub-class covers biomass heat systems producing useful thermal output.
Biogas / Landfill Gas Eligible
New landfill gas and methane facilities qualify as Class I. Pre-2006 landfill gas ≤10 MW qualifies as Class III.
Municipal Solid Waste Not Eligible
MSW combustion not explicitly listed as an eligible resource class under RSA 362-F.
Geothermal Eligible
Electric geothermal qualifies as Class I. Geothermal heat (useful thermal output) qualifies under the Class I Thermal sub-class.
Storage Not Eligible
Standalone storage does not generate RECs. Paired storage with a Class I source may qualify for the renewable attribute.
Nuclear / Zero-Carbon Not Eligible
Fuel Cell Conditional
Fuel cells using renewable fuel (biogas, methane) qualify as Class I. Natural gas fuel cells do not qualify.
