Programs

Class I RPS (New Renewables)

RPS NH Department of Energy RSA 362-F:3, :4 Applies to: EDC, Retail Supplier
Class I covers new renewable generation from facilities that began operation on or after January 1, 2006 — wind, eligible biomass, methane/landfill gas, geothermal, ocean energy, hydrogen from renewable sources, and new small hydro. The Class I requirement rises to 15.0% of load by 2025 (14.1% in 2024) and holds flat thereafter. Within Class I, a Class I Thermal carve-out (RSA 362-F:3) must be met with useful thermal energy — solar thermal, geothermal heat, eligible biomass thermal, and renewable-methane thermal — and reaches 2.2% of load. Class I Thermal RECs are unique among the New England states.

Legislation

Electric Renewable Portfolio Standard (Laws of 2007, Chapter 26)  enacted 2007
Original RPS enactment establishing the Class I–IV framework
2012 Class I Thermal Amendment (Laws of 2012, Chapter 272)
Created the Class I Thermal carve-out for useful thermal renewable energy; effective 2013–2014
2021 Administrative Transfer (HB 2, 2021)
Transferred RPS administration and the Renewable Energy Fund from the PUC to the new NH Department of Energy, effective July 1, 2021

Generation Requirements

Class I Thermal RECs require metering, verification, and reporting of useful thermal output under DOE protocols. All classes settle through NEPOOL GIS; suppliers short of RECs make an Alternative Compliance Payment (ACP) into the Renewable Energy Fund, with the Class I ACP adjusted annually for inflation.

Class II RPS (New Solar)

RPS NH Department of Energy RSA 362-F:3, :4 Applies to: EDC, Retail Supplier
Class II is the solar-specific tier covering new solar electric facilities that began operation on or after January 1, 2006. The requirement reaches 0.7% of load by 2025 and holds flat thereafter. Class II carries the highest Alternative Compliance Payment of the NH classes, reflecting the higher cost of distributed solar.

Legislation

Electric Renewable Portfolio Standard (Laws of 2007, Chapter 26)  enacted 2007
Established the Class II solar tier

Class III RPS (Existing Biomass / Methane)

RPS NH Department of Energy RSA 362-F:3, :4 Applies to: EDC, Retail Supplier
Class III covers existing (pre-January 1, 2006) eligible biomass facilities of 25 MW or less and existing methane sources. The statutory requirement is 8.0% of load and has held at 8.0% through 2024. RSA 362-F:3 lets the Department adjust the Class III obligation downward in some years based on market conditions; the figures shown are the statutory 8.0%. Class III RECs have historically traded heavily into Connecticut's Class I market.

Legislation

Electric Renewable Portfolio Standard (Laws of 2007, Chapter 26)  enacted 2007
Established the Class III existing-biomass/methane tier
2017 Clean Energy Jobs and Opportunity Act (Laws of 2017, Chapter 226)
Raised the Class III Alternative Compliance Payment and directed Renewable Energy Fund support to low- and moderate-income customers

Class IV RPS (Existing Small Hydro)

RPS NH Department of Energy RSA 362-F:3, :4 Applies to: EDC, Retail Supplier
Class IV covers existing small hydroelectric facilities of 5 MW or less that began operation before January 1, 2006 and that have installed required fish passage / meet applicable environmental conditions. The requirement is 1.5% of load and holds flat.

Legislation

Electric Renewable Portfolio Standard (Laws of 2007, Chapter 26)  enacted 2007
Established the Class IV existing-small-hydro tier
