Renewable Energy Standard (RES)

RPS RI RIPUC R.I. Gen. Laws § 39-26 Applies to: National Grid RI, Retail Supplier
Rhode Island's standard is called the Renewable Energy Standard (RES). The 2022 amendment set a goal of 100% renewable electricity offset by 2033. Compliance is administered by InClime, Inc. as contractor to RIPUC.

Legislation

Renewable Energy Standard Act (P.L. 2004, Chapter 205)  enacted 2004
Original RES enactment
2016 RES Extension Amendment (H 7413A / S 2185A)
Extended target to 38.5% renewable energy by 2035
2022 100% Renewable Electricity Standard (HB 7277A / SB 2274Aaa)
Required 100% renewable electricity offset by 2033; complemented by Act on Climate net-zero goal

Long-Term Contracting Standard for Renewable Energy

PROCUREMENT RI RIPUC / OER R.I. Gen. Laws § 39-26.1 Applies to: National Grid RI
Requires the electric distribution company to enter long-term contracts (up to 15 years) for renewable energy. Originally targeted 90 MW of capacity. Provided the contractual mechanism for the Block Island Wind Farm (America's first offshore wind farm, commissioned 2016) and subsequent offshore wind procurements.

Legislation

Long-Term Contracting Standard for Renewable Energy (enacted 2009)  enacted 2009
Established long-term contracting obligation; 90 MW capacity target; specific provisions for offshore wind in federal waters

Procurement Requirements

National Grid RI (the distribution company) issues competitive solicitations for renewable energy contracts of up to 15 years. Contracts subject to RIPUC review and approval. Capacity adjusted by technology-specific capacity factors. Block Island Wind Farm (30 MW, 2016) and subsequent solicitations fulfilled under this authority.

Renewable Energy Growth (REG) Program

PROCUREMENT RI RIPUC / National Grid RI R.I. Gen. Laws § 39-26.6 Applies to: National Grid RI
A tariff-based incentive program for distributed renewable generation — RI's equivalent of a feed-in tariff. Generators receive long-term fixed-price tariff agreements rather than selling RECs into the market. Original target was 160 MW (2015–2019). Program continues under RIPUC oversight.

Legislation

Distributed Generation Growth Act (P.L. 2014, Chapters 200 & 216)  enacted 2014
Created REG program as a tariff-based distributed generation incentive; 160 MW initial target 2015–2019
2023 Net Metering Amendment (Chapter 300, S 0684 A)
Updated net metering provisions affecting REG program administration

Procurement Requirements

Generators enter long-term fixed-price tariff agreements with National Grid RI. Pricing set through RIPUC proceedings. Annual solicitation cycles. Projects must be grid-interconnected distributed generation.
